Impact SE Control

Settings for the Redragon M908 Impact SE, right in your browser.

Get started

  1. Plug in your Impact SE and close Redragon's own app if it's open.
  2. Click Connect mouse, pick Impact SE in the box that pops up, then click Connect.
  3. Change what you like. Nothing reaches the mouse until you click Save to mouse.

Settings are stored on the mouse itself, so they keep working on any computer. Before your first save, this page keeps a copy of your current settings so you can always go back.

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Please open this page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer.

Side buttons

Laid out like the side of the mouse. Tip: F13–F24 are real keys no keyboard has, so games can bind them without clashing with your keyboard.

Top buttons and wheel

DPI stages

The DPI buttons step through the stages that are switched on. 100 to 10,000, in steps of 100.

Light color

Effects like breathing and rainbow aren't supported yet.

Polling rate

How often the mouse reports to the computer. 1000 Hz is smoothest.
